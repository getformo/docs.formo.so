## What is Formo?

**[Formo](https://formo.so) makes analytics and attribution simple [for DeFi apps](https://formo.so/blog/we-built-formo-for-onchain-builders)** so you can focus on growth. Get the best of web, product, and onchain analytics in one place. 

<Frame caption="The analytics and data platform for DeFi.">
	<img src="/images/intro.png" alt="Formo" />
</Frame>

Formo is a unified product analytics, marketing attribution, and wallet intelligence platform built for crypto and DeFi apps. It helps product, growth, and marketing teams understand the complete user journey, from the first website visit to transactions, retention, and revenue.

Developers can integrate Formo quickly using lightweight SDKs, powerful APIs, SQL access, webhooks, and flexible data exports, making it easy to build analytics workflows for modern crypto apps.

## Key Features

### [Product Analytics](/features/product-analytics/overview)

Unified web and product analytics designed for crypto product and marketing teams:
- 📊 **Measure KPIs.** Track visitor counts, DAU, WAU, MAU, transactions, revenue, and retention. Measure product engagement and growth over time.
- 🎯 **Marketing attribution.** Identify the channels and campaigns that drive real onchain activity. Understand where top users come from and measure ROI. 
- 🏃‍➡️ **Optimize your funnel.** Track key touchpoints across the full user journey from offchain to onchain: pageviews, wallet connects, and transactions.
- 📈 **Charts and dashboards.** Visualize data into insights with custom charts and reports with SQL or AI.

### [Wallet Intelligence](/features/wallet-intelligence/overview)

Identify and activate your high-value users with unified user profiles:
-  🕵️‍♂️ **Wallet profiles.** Turn anonymous wallets into rich user profiles based on onchain and offchain data. Track usage of specific, high-value wallets on your crypto app.
-  👥 **Audience insights.** Get comprehensive insights into your audience including Sybil scores, top apps, tokens, chains, net worth, revenue, and retention.
-  🤩 **User segmentation.** Use precise targeting based on wallet holdings, DeFi positions, socials, and in-app activity.

### [Token Gated Forms](/features/token-gated-forms/form-builder)

Launch waitlists, forms, and surveys for your community:
-  🔐 **Token gating.** Verify wallets, token balances, proof-of-personhood, and more.
-  ✅ **Verify socials.** Verify X, Discord, Farcaster, and Lens handles.
-  🎨 **Template library.** Choose from a library of form templates or build your own.

## Privacy Friendly

**Formo is designed with privacy in mind.** No third-party cookies, no personal data. Just clear, privacy-friendly insights.

- No personal data is collected. Formo does not use third-party cookies and never collect information such as IP or device IDs that could be used to fingerprint a user.
- The data we collect belongs to you. No data is shared with third parties.

Here's a comparison of how Formo compares to other analytics tools:

| Data point | Formo | Google Analytics 4 | Plausible | Umami |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| IP address stored | No | Yes (anonymized) | No | No |
| Device fingerprinting | No | Yes | No | No |
| Third-party cookies | No | Yes | No | No |
| Full user-agent stored | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| Cross-site tracking | No | Yes | No | No |
| Requires cookie consent banner | No | Yes (in EU) | No | No |
| GDPR-compliant by default | Yes | No (requires configuration) | Yes | Yes |
| Open source SDK | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Self-hostable | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Blockchain-native analytics | Yes | No | No | No |

Formo collects as little information as is necessary to give you actionable analytics for onchain apps. [See what we collect](/data/what-we-collect).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is Formo different from Google Analytics?">
    Formo is analytics designed for onchain apps. Unlike Google Analytics, Formo natively tracks key touchpoints such as wallet connects, transactions, and smart contract events alongside standard web analytics like pageviews and sessions. It also handles [onchain attribution](/data/attribution) and [wallet intelligence](/features/wallet-intelligence/overview) for crypto and DeFi teams.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What chains does Formo support?">
    Formo supports all major chains across Ethereum and Solana ecosystems. See the full list of [supported chains](/chains/overview). Don't see your chain? Let us know.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which frontend frameworks does Formo support?">
    Formo works with all major frontend frameworks: React, Vue, Svelte, Next.js, Remix, Astro, and vanilla JavaScript. Formo works with industry-standard libraries such as wagmi and viem. See [SDKs](/sdks/web) for framework-specific integration guides and examples.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Formo SDK track?">
    After installing the SDK, Formo automatically tracks the full user journey from offchai to onchain including page views, sessions, referrer sources, UTM parameters, referral codes, wallet connects, disconnects, chain switches, transactions, and signatures. See [events](/data/events/overview) and [what we collect](/data/what-we-collect) for more information.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track events from my backend?">
    Yes. Use the [Server-side SDK](/sdks/server) and [API](/api/overview) to send data from the backend.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often is my data updated?">
    All data is ingested and displayed in real time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
